No documentation examined establishes a sanctioned machine-readable feed for these OEMs. During each pilot refresh record the explicit model year, retrieval date, variant/engine package, market, units and the source’s pricing exclusions. Retain storage or publication copies only under the applicable grant. Keep the source URL, share independently written editorial notes, and direct readers to a manufacturer for missing values or material conflicts.

## Phase 3B pilot

[Sea-Doo current US lineup](https://sea-doo.brp.com/us/en/models.html) and [2026 US spec book](https://sea-doo.brp.com/content/dam/global/en/sea-doo/my26/documents/brochures/SEA-MY26-SpecBook-ENUS-LR.pdf) cover current and archived PWC plus current Switch variants. [Yamaha Boats](https://www.yamahaboats.com/build-and-price/) and [2027 WaveRunner recreation lineup](https://www.yamahawaverunners.com/waverunner/series/recreation/) supplement model pages. Some Yamaha aggregate/price pages redirect to a GDPR page from the research environment. Exact sources by configuration are in the local review catalog.

Expansion candidates include [Kawasaki STX 160X](https://www.kawasaki.com/en-us/watercraft/jet-ski/sit-down/jet-ski-stx-160/2027-jet-ski-stx-160x), [Scarab US boats](https://www.scarabjetboats.com/us/Boat), [Sun Tracker pontoons](https://www.suntrackerboats.com/pontoon-boats.html), and [Bennington M series](https://www.benningtonmarine.com/en-us/m-series/m/). Verify exact model year, installed engine and price package separately for each observation. Manufacturer approvals and provider permissions are open.
