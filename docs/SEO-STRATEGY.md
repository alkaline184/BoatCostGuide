# SEO strategy

The homepage targets introductory boat loan and ownership budgeting questions with a working tool and server-rendered guidance. Supporting Phase 1 pages explain the product, methods, privacy and terms. Internal links point to published routes or useful homepage sections.

The canonical origin is `https://boatcostguide.com`. Keep public metadata, the sitemap and robots sitemap pointer on this origin. Verify canonical URL, title, description, Open Graph, Twitter card and structured data when adding a page. Preview deployments use a noindex response header. Protect previews through Vercel when the project is connected.

Give future tools distinct jobs and substantive examples before indexing them. A dedicated payment calculator should answer loan-specific questions with a fuller schedule and comparisons. An ownership calculator should help estimate expense categories and distinguish annual budgeting from economic costs. Avoid publishing pages that repeat the homepage with a different boat type in the title.

After launch, submit the canonical sitemap via Search Console. Use indexing, engagement and available field performance evidence to prioritize improvements. Verify title and content changes against real search intent.

Phase 2 gives fixed-rate payment and amortization intent to the dedicated loan calculator. The homepage targets broader budgeting and ownership planning. Affordability, ownership cost and payoff calculations have distinct canonical routes, preview-aware indexing, server-rendered copy, breadcrumbs, and reciprocal links. Amortization is part of the loan route.

Phase 3A shifts the homepage to broader boat discovery and ownership intent. Boat-type educational disclosures are server-rendered on the homepage. The model and comparison prototypes are development-only, noindex and absent from the sitemap. See the [indexing criteria](MARINE-ARCHITECTURE.md) before creating public catalog routes.

## Phase 3B indexing

The sitemap uses the same rights and verification gate as production model pages. Maker and type pages require two distinct families. The reviewed catalog and arbitrary filter and comparison queries remain noindex. Family URLs are canonical across variant query states. Product offers, review scores and merchant markup are absent. The public Boat Explorer is noindex while it serves the publication-pending directory.
