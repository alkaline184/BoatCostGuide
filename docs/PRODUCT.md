# Product

BoatCostGuide helps US boat buyers compare financing scenarios and budget for ownership before they commit to a purchase. Buyers should be able to enter actual quotes, understand what drives the estimate and see the limitations in plain language.

Phase 1 includes a homepage calculator with amount or percent down payments, loan and cash purchase scenarios, a breakdown of recurring costs, and guidance on building a budget. The About, Methodology, Privacy and Terms routes provide context and accountability.

Core tools stay free without registration. Future referrals should be contextual and clearly disclosed. Any actual rates, endorsements or financial partner relationships require current supporting evidence.
