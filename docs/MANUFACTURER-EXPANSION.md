# Phase 3B.2 manufacturer acquisition

### Measured extraction and editorial work

The 29 curated submissions passed automated validation in roughly 5 ms in a run that included file reads. Acquisition discovered and retained 15 Sun Tracker packages, seven Kawasaki exact-year spec sheets, three Sea-Doo archive configurations and four archived Yamaha WaveRunner configurations. Two discounted Sun Tracker Sportfish cards shown under the 2027 heading were actually labeled 2026 and were left outside this batch. The base package for the 2027 Party Barge 20 uses the US seating figure of ten from the detailed spec. Its Canadian capacity is eight. The accepted batch required no validator corrections. This measures finalized inputs, after human reconciliation and source selection. [Sun Tracker lineup](https://www.suntrackerboats.com/pontoon-boats.html) · [Party Barge 20 details](https://www.suntrackerboats.com/recreational-pontoons/party-barge-20-dlx.html)

Five sample variants from Sun Tracker, Sea-Doo and Kawasaki were independently checked against the relevant lineup, product page or spec sheet for 14 overlapping measurements. All 14 agreed after normalization within 0.75 in for dimensions, 3 lb for weight or 0.2 US gal for fuel. The cross-check pass took approximately 32 elapsed seconds across batched source lookups, verification and attaching the checks to known identities. This is about six seconds per already-identified sample, with sources already available. It does not measure the time to discover and research a new record. Four WaveRunners use one official archived brochure and were outside the independent corroboration sample. [Kawasaki exact-year sheet](https://www.kawasaki.com/en-us/products/ProductSpecSheetPDF/2027-jet-ski-stx-160x) · [Kawasaki product detail](https://www.kawasaki.com/en-us/watercraft/jet-ski/sit-down/jet-ski-stx-160/2027-jet-ski-stx-160x) · [Sea-Doo GTX archive](https://sea-doo.brp.com/us/en/models/previous-models/2025/gtx.html) · [Sea-Doo GTX spec sheet](https://sea-doo.brp.com/content/dam/global/en/sea-doo/my25/documents/specs-sheets/na/en/SEA-MY25-TOUR-GTX-SPEC-ENNA-Page-PDFx.pdf)

A warmed timing run over 100 repetitions per brand measured median normalization and validation of 110 µs for Kawasaki, 118 µs for Sea-Doo, 174 µs for Sun Tracker and 34 µs for WaveRunner. These timings exclude file reads and editorial work. Normalization and validation are shared code across all sources. Manufacturer profile effort is part of this implementation rather than separately timed per brand. No broad human time savings are inferred from the importer runtime. For planning, reserve 8–16 minutes per new exact configuration including identity, facts, quote basis and QA once the source format is known. The estimates exclude image review, licensing, and periodic refresh.

## Permissions and next phase

Kawasaki restricts automated collection and commercial use. White River covers Sun Tracker and Tracker and explicitly restricts automated collecting and reuse. BRP and Yamaha restrict site-material reuse. Scarab restricts server copying and use of site content. These facts are maintained for local review with their sources, original annotations, and no image copies. Written permissions are unresolved. [Kawasaki policies](https://www.kawasaki.com/en-us/about/legal-policies) · [White River terms](https://www.whiterivermg.com/terms.html) · [BRP legal notice](https://sea-doo.brp.com/us/en/legal-notice.html) · [Yamaha terms](https://www.yamahaboats.com/terms-and-conditions/) · [Scarab terms](https://www.scarabjetboats.com/intl/terms-and-conditions)

Expand another 50 after approving source scope and piloting rights-cleared imagery. Investigate Scarab exact US configuration sheets, paired prices and permissions. Tracker’s fishing boat lines require a catalog category decision because the current shopping taxonomy is personal watercraft, jet boats and pontoons. Secure at least one manufacturer-approved data and media intake path before adding network automation. [Tracker model discovery](https://www.trackerboats.com/aluminum-fishing-boats.html) · [Scarab model discovery](https://www.scarabjetboats.com/us/Boat) · [Scarab information guides](https://www.scarabjetboats.com/us/product-information-guides)
