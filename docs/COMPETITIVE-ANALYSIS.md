## Product opportunities

- Connect a monthly spending target to actual published starting prices so a shopper can move directly from budget to shortlist.
- Preserve exact variant, year, price basis and field provenance across card, research and comparison. Keep tested performance configuration-specific when it is available.
- Make the five-year cash outlay, remaining balance and resale-adjusted result separately legible. Allow shared and per-model assumptions and share only variant identifiers.
- Test phone comparison with a visible scroll area and keyboard navigation. Preserve crawlable model, category and maker links once coverage clears editorial and rights review.
