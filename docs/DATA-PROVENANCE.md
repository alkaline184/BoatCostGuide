# Marine data provenance

Each displayed claim carries an official US manufacturer URL, date checked, model year, and verification state. The private source-document ledger also records source type, commercial display, indexing and retention decisions, an approval reference, and expiration where applicable. Specification observations preserve the captured value or text and unit, normalized value and unit, and predecessor when a new observation supersedes the current one. Older source batches without a raw value retain the normalized observation as the available evidence.

Prices have their own observation ledger with USD amount, price type, date, historical status, package inclusions and exclusions, and source. Archives remain available to reviewers. Current-price scenarios consume valid dated starting prices and leave missing prices blank. A manufacturer’s starting price does not establish final transaction price.

The local model and comparison views attach source links to known specification and current price values. Manufacturer or family-level evidence is retained separately from variant evidence. Deck width and hull beam are distinct evidence, as are dry weight and trailer-inclusive stored weight.

The Yamaha expansion points to the exact model comparison sheet using PDF page fragments. The model year links to the archive cover. The archived boat weight is omitted where the manufacturer does not define its load condition.
