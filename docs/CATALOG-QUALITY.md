Observations use specific official model and configuration pages and the 2026 Sea-Doo US specification book. Sea-Doo Spark archived and current starting prices lacked sufficient reconciliation with exact configuration observations. Yamaha's yacht-certified craft are missing a numeric seat limit. Published horsepower figures are used only when attributable to the installed configuration. Category artwork serves as a labeled illustration.

BRP's legal notice restricts reuse of website material and Yamaha's terms cover both boat and WaveRunner websites. All pilot facts remain under local review pending a commercial display, retention and indexing decision. The catalog contains source links and independent factual observations. Manufacturer imagery and marketing copy are excluded. [BRP legal notice](https://sea-doo.brp.com/us/en/legal-notice.html) · [Yamaha terms](https://www.yamahaboats.com/terms-and-conditions/)
